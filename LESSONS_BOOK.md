## 1. Short TLDR (about two minutes)

Mark’s job is a bot that trades **his** way through MetaTrader 5, on any symbol at the same time, with **daily target and daily risk typed each day** (not baked into the mind), until **100 real consistent days** are measured. Done order is always: **trade win rate first**, then **day target reached (day hits)**, then **mean dollars**. If day hits rise while trade win rate falls, that is **stamping the day**, not learning to trade — revert.

Two separate products must stay separate:

- **Box keep student** (meta-learn folder on Mark’s personal computer): US30 learner with locks, cameras, credit climb, Lock 9 wait mask. Cloud does not train. Resume only — never start fresh without Mark.
- **Desktop version 5** (day-target harness on the Desktop): printable 100-day hidden exam with random typed 3–35 percent target and 1–6 percent risk. Best island after card h99: win rate about **31.7 percent**, day hits **26 of 100**, day risk spent **6**. Not Done.
- **Desktop version 4** is an older leftover-pay school; almost every late card failed the hidden exam.
- **Shared doctrine** (OBJECTIVE / METHOD / THINKING / REWARDS): cameras are eyes in observation, not reward badges; makeup before pay; physics prizes stay off while align about **93 percent** and win rate about **29 percent** stamp pattern.

Box keep timeline in one breath: Credit assign KEEP → Eyes cameras KEEP (observation size 112→266) → closed profit and loss climb KEEP (epoch 44 win rate 24.12) → weigh log KEEP on win rate (epoch 47) → wait-collapse (zero-open days ~230+) → best selective book **epoch 199** (3 day hits / 33 trades / win rate **36.36 percent** / zero-open days ~214) — **weights never frozen, overwritten** → Lock 9 wait mask from epoch 200 = spray (~5.5–7.5 thousand trades, zero-open days 8, win rate ~29–33 percent) — **failed the epoch-199 gate** but Mark left it on; hold Credit items 13 and 14 → money day-grade credit mode briefly, then **Mark revert to closed profit and loss on 2026-09-05**.

Neither product is Done as of these sources.
